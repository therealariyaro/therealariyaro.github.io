The Iranian people have for decades been trapped under a tyrannical Islamist theocracy that has left them completely oppressed and humiliated.
Iran is a multicultural, multiethnic, multireligious nation blessed with a beautiful culture, but it is being ruled by self-proclaimed "godmen" who rule through stone-age beliefs and crush the public with senseless cruelty.
The mere sight of a woman's hair sends the rulers into a rage, sending "morality police" to beat civilians into submission. What barbarians like this have any right to rule?

Iran is a country of contradictions. The society and the state are incompatible. A highly educated, culturally rich people ruled by tyrants who claim some divine right to rule enshrined through scripture written over a thousand years ago.
A conflict between these two forces is not only possible but inevitable. And for years, we have seen this conflict play out, hidden and open and hidden again, until it finally breaks out.
As I write this, Iranians are protesting and dying on the streets for their most basic human rights while their rulers live like kings, proclaiming their moral purity to the people they butcher like rats.

But that conflict will resolve, and it will not be at the hands of the completely incompetent and immoral rulers who have destroyed their country's economy, environment, and tortured its people mercilessly.
The protests in 2022 after Mahsa Amini's death were ultimately unsuccessful, but they sent a powerful message. One that the pigs up top have been foolish enough to ignore, or at most to consider lightly.
This is the fruit they reap. Resistance is a necessity, and the people of Iran are responding loudly, led one by the other with courage they were cautious not to show publicly for the last few years.

Will the protests succeed today? No one can tell. I pray they do, and I pray we see not just feeble reform, but a true revolution that purges the corrupt, backwards mullahs from any aspect of power, like they should have been.
But no matter what happens, the tide is turning, and the message is clear. Nothing less than total destruction of the clergy's power in Iran will save the country. But the revolution will succeed one day or another, and the people's will grows stronger with each day.
The sacrifices of thousands, from Mosadegh in 1953 to Jina Amini in 2022, will not be in vain. The wound that has festered for years in the nation's fabric will finally heal, at any cost. And the brave citizens are willing to pay whatever cost is necessary to claim the only true future they can have, one without the greed of the bastards in power. People should not fear their governments, governments should fear their people.

And victory will bring prosperity to this nation. Freedom for the first time since a lifetime's worth of years. Freedom to live long and prosper, to follow the faith you choose, or none at all.

They have to win thousands of times, but you only have to win once. It is time for the people to go forward, and reclaim the freedom they have every right to, but which was stolen away from them for the delusion and greed of the few in power.

To all those outside of Iran, we must denounce the theocracy in power and lend our ears, join our voices to the chorus of those bleeding and struggling for freedom.
Stay safe, stay fighting, and never give up. Live free or die.

Let 2026 be the year tyrants will dread to even think about.
Death to all parties of God. Jin Jiyan Azadi.
